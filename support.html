Common questions

Does FibToMe detect lies?

No. It measures how consistent the behavioural signals in a recording are with one another and reports that as a score. It is an educational tool. A score is not evidence that anyone lied, and you should not use it to judge anyone. See the Terms for uses that are not permitted.

Why did I get "No score" or "Not enough signal"?

The app declines to show a score when it measured too little to say anything — for example a clip with no speech, a face that was mostly out of frame, or very poor lighting. Showing a number in that situation would suggest a finding that does not exist. Record for the full duration, speak normally, and face your light source rather than having it behind you.

Why does my score sit around 50?

Fifty is the midpoint the score moves toward when confidence in the measurement is low. It means "little information", not "borderline result".

The heart rate looks wrong, or does not appear

The pulse figure is an experimental estimate read from small colour changes in facial skin. It only appears when the signal passes a quality check, so on many recordings it will be absent — that is intended. It is not a medical measurement, is not accurate enough to act on, and does not affect your score. Talking, movement, uneven lighting and skin tone all affect it.

What happens to my video?

It stays on your phone. To analyse it, the video and audio are sent to Google's Gemini service, and we request deletion as soon as the result comes back. FibToMe's own servers never receive it. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete a recording?

Long-press the scan on the Home screen or in your Profile and choose Delete video. The newest scan can also be deleted from its ⋯ menu. Deleting removes the video and its report from your device.

Deleting a scan did not restore my monthly allowance

That is deliberate. The allowance counts analyses performed. Once an analysis has run the work is done, so deleting the recording afterwards does not give it back. The allowance resets at the start of each month.

How do I withdraw permission for AI processing?

Settings → Legal & Privacy → AI Data Processing. The screen shows whether you have accepted and when. You can review the disclosure again or withdraw. Withdrawing stops future analyses; existing reports are unaffected and can be deleted individually.

How do I delete my account and everything in it?

Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account, your stored documents, your local videos, and destroys the key that links any research contributions to you. If part of it cannot complete, deletion stops and tells you rather than leaving data behind.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting FibToMe does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are requested from Apple.

Restore purchases

Open the subscription screen in the app and choose Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID for an active subscription.

Reporting a problem or a Community post

Email info@eresonance.ch with a description and, if relevant, the approximate time you saw it. For crashes, telling us your device model and iOS version helps considerably.

Accessibility

FibToMe supports Dynamic Type and VoiceOver, and controls are sized for reliable touch. If something is unusable with your accessibility settings, please tell us — that is a bug worth fixing.
