2. What the app does, stated plainly

FibToMe records a short video of a person speaking and produces a behavioural signal score: a description of how consistent the signals it could measure were with one another, across face, voice and language.

What that score is not:

  • Not a probability. It is not calibrated against known-truth data and cannot express the odds of anything.
  • Not a verdict. A low score does not mean someone lied. A high score does not mean they told the truth.
  • Not validated. No accuracy claim is made for it, because no accuracy study supports one.
  • Not diagnostic. It says nothing about anyone's mental or physical health.

Decades of research find that behavioural cues to deception are weak and inconsistent, and that people judging truthfulness unaided perform close to chance. FibToMe is built to teach that nuance, not to override it.
